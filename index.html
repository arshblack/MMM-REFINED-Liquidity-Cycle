How research becomes a public record

Click a stage. This publishes the vocabulary so trade updates are legible — not how the EA sequences or decides anything.

A setup identified on TradingView or in analysis. An observation to examine, outside the EA execution record.
break-even = stop moved to entry partial close = part of the position taken off invalidation = the read no longer holds setup expiry = conditions met, follow-through didn’t

Research is not execution. A setup enters this record only when the MT5 EA confirms a trade. A record of what the system did — not a claim about what it will do.

Read the observations and failure states
  1. Research setup: A setup identified on TradingView or in analysis. An observation to examine, outside the EA execution record.
  2. EA confirmed: The MT5 Expert Advisor confirms execution. This is the line where research becomes part of the public record.
  3. T1 protected: A reporting label for a confirmed first objective or protection event. It does not promise that a target will be reached or specify a stop change.
  4. T2 managed: A label for a confirmed management event. Read the actual update for what changed; no management rule is implied here.
  5. T3 / closed: The final stage, or the position closes. The outcome is recorded either way — including when it’s a loss.
  6. Evidence archived: The completed record is archived, timestamped. A record of what the system did — never a claim about what it will do.

A setup can expire or invalidate before execution. A confirmed position can close at a loss without reaching any target. These are vocabulary labels, not a mandatory sequence.

Break-even describes a stop level; spread, fees and slippage can still produce a net loss.
